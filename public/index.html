How Uplivanta works

Simple for people. Careful with money and trust.

🧾
1. Submit a real bill

Explain the amount, due date and biller. Hide account numbers and sensitive details.

🔎
2. Verify the request

Moderators review identity, bill evidence, payee and duplicate or suspicious activity.

🌐
3. Find willing supporters

Share the approved request and let interested people discover it through public links or opt-in profiles.

🔐
4. Settle safely

Use a licensed, approved payment partner and a documented refund and dispute process.
